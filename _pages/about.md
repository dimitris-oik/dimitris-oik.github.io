Hello! I'm Dimitris (feel free to call me Dimitri), a final-year PhD student in 
[Computer Science](https://www.cs.jhu.edu/) at Johns Hopkins University ([JHU](https://www.jhu.edu/)), 
advised by Prof. [Nicolas Loizou](https://nicolasloizou.github.io/). I expect to graduate in 
Spring 2027 and I am **on the job market** for research scientist positions starting in 2027.

I'm broadly interested in large-scale optimization and machine learning. My current 
research focuses on adaptive step-size methods and sharpness-aware minimization, with 
applications to deep learning and large language model training.

Before JHU, I earned a BSc in [Mathematics](https://en.math.uoa.gr/) from the 
National and Kapodistrian University of Athens ([NKUA](https://en.uoa.gr/)), and MSc degrees in 
[Data Science and Machine Learning](https://dsml.ece.ntua.gr/) from the 
National Technical University of Athens ([NTUA](https://www.ntua.gr/en/); [thesis](https://dimitris-oik.github.io/files/ntua_thesis.pdf)) 
and in [Mathematics](https://www.uni-goettingen.de/en/faculty+of+mathematics+and+computer+science/20491.html) 
from the [University of G&ouml;ttingen](https://www.uni-goettingen.de/en/1.html) ([thesis](https://dimitris-oik.github.io/files/goettingen_thesis.pdf)).
